Last updated: November 15, 2026

Reply STOP to end texts at any time. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies by appointment.

What these texts are

Businesses that use First Access send appointment-related text messages to their clients through First Access. Texts come from the business you booked with and may include:

  • Booking confirmations with a link to your visit card
  • Prep reminders before your visit
  • Aftercare after your visit
  • A request to share a review
  • A reminder when it's time to book your next visit

For your privacy, texts refer to “your visit” and never name the treatment. Details appear only inside your card.

Consent

You receive these texts only if you agreed to get texts from the business you booked with. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

How often

Message frequency varies with your appointments, typically a few messages around each visit and a reminder when your next visit is due.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. First Access and the business don't charge you for these texts.

Stopping texts

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts from that business through First Access. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed. To start again, reply START or ask the business.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email jomar@bowtiefunnel.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your mobile number and messages are handled under our Privacy Policy. We never sell your phone number or share it with third parties for their marketing.
