Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run Concierge Links for the businesses that use First Access, and nothing more.
  • We never sell personal information.
  • One business's client data is never shared with or used for another business.
  • Clients can stop texts at any time by replying STOP.

This policy explains how [Company legal name] (“First Access,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you visit firstaccess.io, join our waitlist, or use First Access as a business or as a client of a business.

Information we collect

From businesses and waitlist sign-ups. Your name, business name, email address, city, the booking system you use, and anything else you choose to send us.

From connected booking systems. When a business connects a booking system, First Access reads appointment details, client first names and mobile numbers. Access is read-only: we never change or cancel bookings, and we never receive payment card details.

From clients using a Concierge Link. Which steps of the Concierge Link are opened, prep and aftercare checklists, product links clicked, add-ons chosen, tips started, review drafts and rebooking choices.

Automatically. Basic technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use information

  • To send each client the Concierge Link and texts their business has approved: confirmations, prep, aftercare, reminders, review requests and rebooking notes.
  • To show businesses how their Concierge Links perform, including add-ons, rebookings and product sales that came through them.
  • To help businesses find products and affiliate programs that fit their services.
  • To contact waitlist members about access, setup and updates.
  • To keep First Access secure, fix problems and meet legal obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With the business a client booked with. The business sees activity on its own clients' Concierge Links.
  • With service providers who help us run First Access, such as hosting, text-message delivery and email, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • With affiliate programs and stores, only to the extent a client clicks a product link and leaves First Access for the seller's site. Purchases happen on the seller's site, under the seller's own privacy policy.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

Text messages

Texts go only to clients who agreed to receive them from their business. Texts refer to “your visit” and never name the treatment; details appear only inside the client's Concierge Link. Reply STOP to end texts or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as a business uses First Access and as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. When a business leaves, we delete or anonymize its client data within [90] days unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Texts. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them.
  • Email. Use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you are a client of a business that uses First Access, you can also contact that business directly. To make a request, email jomar@bowtiefunnel.com.

Children

First Access is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email or on the site.
