Questions owners ask

Will my clients feel sold to?

No. Recommendations show up only where they fit the service and the day of recovery, with an honest disclosure. It reads like the care you already give, not a sales pitch.

What is First Access?

A better experience around every appointment, and more revenue from it. Every booking sends your client a First Access Concierge Link with prep before the visit, aftercare after it, product picks from you, a review step and their next booking.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. They tap their Concierge Link in a text and it opens in their phone's browser, with no login. They use the same link from booking through rebooking.

Will it message my clients without asking?

No. Only clients who agreed to texts get them, and only what you've approved. You approve prep and aftercare once per service, and every product pick, review reply and rebooking note before it goes out.

Which booking systems does it work with?

Square and Google Calendar at launch. First Access reads appointments, first names and mobile numbers. It never changes a booking and never sees payment details. Founding studios choose which systems we connect next.

What kinds of businesses is it for?

Any small beauty, wellness or fitness business where a professional performs the service: salons, barbers, nails, lash and brow, med spas, spas, massage, physical therapy, chiropractic, yoga, pilates, personal training and more. Founding spots open first for med spas and salons.

How do product recommendations earn money?

You earn affiliate commissions through a brand's or retailer's program, or you sell the products you already stock. Your Concierge finds the products and programs that fit each service. Every pick carries a disclosure, as the FTC requires.

How do reviews work?

Every client gets the same chance to post, whatever their rating. The review helper starts from the client's own words, and low ratings also get a private line to you. Your Concierge drafts replies to public reviews in your voice, and you approve each one. Yelp discourages asking for reviews, so Yelp is a separate switch, and the setting explains the risk before you turn it on.

How much does it cost?

Nothing until First Access has earned you $100. Your dashboard adds up the add-ons, rebookings and sales of your own products that come through your Concierge Links. Once that passes $100, the $99/mo plan starts.

When can I start?

Founding studios get access first. We'll email you before your setup opens. Joining the list costs nothing.
