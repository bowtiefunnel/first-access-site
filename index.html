Questions owners ask

Before you sign up

How do I earn from product recommendations?

You join affiliate programs such as Amazon Associates, ShopMy, LTK or a brand's own program. First Access helps you find and apply to them, and the programs pay commissions to you directly.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. They tap a link in a text and the card opens in their phone's browser. They use the same link from booking through rebooking.

Will this mess with my booking system?

No. First Access reads appointments, first names and mobile numbers. It can't change or cancel a booking, and it never sees payment details.

Is it allowed to ask clients for reviews?

Yes, if every client gets the same chance to post, whatever their rating. That's how First Access works. Yelp discourages asking for reviews, so Yelp is a separate switch, and the setting explains the risk before you turn it on.

My booking system isn't Square. Can I still join?

Yes. Google Calendar works with any studio, and founding studios tell us which systems to connect next. Pick yours in the form below.

When do I start paying?

Only after First Access has earned you $99. Your dashboard adds up the add-ons, rebookings and product sales that come through your client cards. Once that total passes $99, your $99/mo plan starts. Until then, it's free.

When can I start?

Founding studios get access first. We'll email you before your setup opens. Joining the list costs nothing.
